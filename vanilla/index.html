    🔭 Focal length

    Sets your angle of view — 24 mm captures the whole scene, 200 mm isolates the subject. Longer lenses magnify and shrink depth of field, so backgrounds melt away faster.

    zoom in · 24 → 200 mm
    Zoom / framing↑
    Background blur↑

    🕳️ ƒ-stop (aperture)

    The iris opening. Low ƒ-numbers (ƒ/1.4) pour in light and blur everything off the focus plane; high numbers (ƒ/16+) keep near-and-far sharp but starve the sensor — and past ƒ/16, diffraction softens detail.

    stop down · ƒ/1.4 → ƒ/22
    Light gathered↓
    Depth of field↑
    Background blur↓
    Diffraction↑

    📈 ISO

    Amplifies the signal. Each doubling buys a stop of brightness for dark scenes, but amplifies noise too — grain crawls in from ISO 800 and gets loud at 6400+. Try the 🌙 Night scene and watch it work.

    raise gain · 100 → 25.6k
    Brightness↑
    Grain / noise↑
    Colour & contrast↓

    🎬 Shutter & distance

    Shutter speed trades light for freeze — slower than 1/focal (mm) and handheld shake smears the frame. Subject distance moves the focus plane: everything except the subject re-blurs around it.
